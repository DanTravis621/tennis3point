What it is

Clashpoint is a rotating singles session played under Fast Four rules. You face a series of different opponents and keep playing from start to finish. Nobody is eliminated and nobody sits out.

This one is an introduction to the Clashpoint system. Good Club Standard and above — come ready to compete.

  • Continual play, not knockout
  • A new opponent each round
  • Matches run 20 to 30 minutes
  • You play for the full three hours
  • The rotation is explained in the briefing

How a match is won

Best of three Fast Four sets — first to two sets takes the match. At one set all, a traditional 7-point tiebreak decides it (first to seven, two clear points).

  • First to four games wins the set
  • No advantage: at deuce, one sudden-death point, receiver chooses the side
  • Let serves are played
  • At three games all, a short tiebreak to five points, sudden death at four-all

The two tiebreaks are different: five points to settle a set at 3–3, seven to settle the match at one set all.
