DESCRIPTION
Three hours of fast singles. No knockout - you play right through.

Clashpoint is a rotating singles session played under Fast Four rules. Each match
is best of three Fast Four sets - win two sets and the match is yours - and at
one set all a traditional 7-point tiebreak settles it. Matches run 20 to 30
minutes, so you face a series of different opponents and keep playing from start
to finish.

This one is an introduction to the Clashpoint system. Sixteen places, Good Club
Standard and above - come ready to compete.

HOW A MATCH IS WON
- Best of three Fast Four sets - first to two sets takes the match
- At one set all, a traditional 7-point tiebreak decides it (first to seven, two
  clear points)

FAST FOUR SCORING, WITHIN EACH SET
- First to four games wins the set
- No advantage: at deuce, one sudden-death point, receiver chooses the side
- Let serves are played
- At three games all, a short tiebreak to five points, sudden death at four-all

Note the two tiebreaks are different: five points to settle a set at 3-3, seven
points to settle the match at one set all.

HOW THE SESSION RUNS
- Continual play, not knockout - nobody is eliminated, nobody sits out
- 20 to 30 minutes a match, a new opponent each round
- You play for the full three hours
- The rotation is explained in the briefing before play starts

ON THE DAY

WHO IT IS FOR
Good Club Standard and above. Matches are competitive and played back to back, so you will
get the most out of the day if you are playing singles regularly.
Under 16s welcome alongside an adult.

WHAT TO BRING
Tennis shoes, a racket and water. Balls provided. Courts are outdoors, so dress
for Brighton weather and bring a spare layer.
