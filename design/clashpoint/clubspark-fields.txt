EVENT NAME
Clashpoint - Fast Four Singles

CAPACITY
16

DESCRIPTION (618 characters)
Three hours of fast singles. No knockout - you play right through.

Clashpoint is a rotating singles session played under Fast Four rules. Each match is best of three Fast Four sets - win two sets and the match is yours - and at one set all a traditional 7-point tiebreak settles it. Matches run 20 to 30 minutes, so you face a series of different opponents and keep playing from start to finish. Nobody is eliminated and nobody sits out.

This one is an introduction to the Clashpoint system. Sixteen places, Good Club Standard and above - come ready to compete.

Saturday 19 September, 2-5pm. Preston Park, Brighton.

ADDITIONAL CONFIRMATION (881 characters)
You're in. Here is how the day runs.

HOW A MATCH IS WON
Best of three Fast Four sets - first to two sets takes the match. At one set all, a traditional 7-point tiebreak decides it (first to seven, two clear points).

FAST FOUR SCORING, WITHIN EACH SET
First to four games wins the set. No advantage: at deuce, one sudden-death point, receiver chooses the side. Let serves are played. At three games all, a short tiebreak to five points, sudden death at four-all.

The two tiebreaks are different: five points to settle a set at 3-3, seven to settle the match at one set all.

RUNNING ORDER
2.00pm arrival and briefing. 2.15pm play begins. 4.30pm final round. 5.00pm finish. The rotation is explained in the briefing before play starts.

WHAT TO BRING
Tennis shoes, a racket and water. Balls are provided. Courts are outdoors, so dress for Brighton weather and bring a spare layer.
