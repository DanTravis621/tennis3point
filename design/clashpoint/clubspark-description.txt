DESCRIPTION
Three hours of fast singles. No knockout - you play right through.

Clashpoint is a rotating singles session played under Fast Four rules: first to
four games takes the set, sudden-death point at deuce, tie-break at three-all.
Matches run about 15 minutes, so you face a different opponent every round and
keep playing from start to finish.

This one is an introduction to the Clashpoint system. Sixteen places, all
standards welcome - come and see how it plays.

FAST FOUR SCORING
- First to four games wins the set
- No advantage: at deuce, one sudden-death point, receiver chooses the side
- Let serves are played
- Tie-break at three games all, first to five points, sudden death at four-all

HOW THE SESSION RUNS
- Continual play, not knockout - nobody is eliminated, nobody sits out
- Around 15 minutes a match, a new opponent each round
- You play for the full three hours
- The rotation is explained in the briefing before play starts

ON THE DAY
- 2.00pm: arrival and briefing
- 2.15pm: play begins
- 4.45pm: final round
- 5.00pm: finish

WHO IT IS FOR
All standards welcome, whether you play singles every week or hardly at all.
Under 16s welcome alongside an adult.

WHAT TO BRING
Tennis shoes, a racket and water. Balls provided. Courts are outdoors, so dress
for Brighton weather and bring a spare layer.

Saturday 19 September, 2-5pm. Preston Park, Brighton. Sixteen places.
